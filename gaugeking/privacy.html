Gauge King Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 30, 2026

Last updated: June 30, 2026

Gauge King does not require login, does not require account creation, and does not collect personally identifiable information (PII).

1. Who This Policy Applies To

This policy applies to the Gauge King mobile app and related privacy disclosures for Gauge King.

2. Information We Collect

Gauge King is designed to operate without collecting personal data from users.

  • No account or login is required.
  • No name, email address, phone number, or similar personal identifier is collected by the app.
  • No in-app collection of personally identifiable information (PII).

3. Vehicle and Session Data

Gauge King can read vehicle telemetry data from a compatible adapter to provide dashboard features inside the app. This telemetry data is processed locally on your device for app functionality.

  • Telemetry and session data are used to display gauges, charts, and session summaries in the app.
  • If you export data, it is exported at your direction.
  • Gauge King does not require cloud sync or user accounts to use core features.

4. Data Sharing

Gauge King does not sell personal data and is not designed to share personal data with third parties because the app does not collect PII.

5. Data Retention

Data created by the app (for example local settings or local session files) remains on your device unless you delete the app, clear app data, or export files.

6. Children’s Privacy

Gauge King does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Platform and Third-Party Services

Apple and other platform providers may process limited technical information as part of operating iOS, the App Store, or device diagnostics. Those processes are governed by the platform provider's own privacy terms.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the published Gauge King privacy URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions about Gauge King, use the support contact method listed on the Gauge King App Store listing or the Gauge King website support page.
