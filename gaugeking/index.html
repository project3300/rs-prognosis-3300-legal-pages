What makes GaugeKing different

GaugeKing focuses on clarity, trust, and driving context. Each reading is shown with practical visual hierarchy so you can glance and decide quickly.

Custom Dashboard Layouts

Select your gauges, reorder them, and tune the dashboard style to your preferences. You can keep it minimal for track sessions or rich for full telemetry monitoring.

Reliable Telemetry Path

Gauge data can be sourced from live OBD-II readings or synthetic test feeds, helping you validate setups and demos even when the vehicle is not connected.

Performance-Oriented Visuals

Track-friendly themes, readable numeric cards, and configurable accent colors make the dashboard easier to read under real usage conditions.

Motorsport Shift Guidance

Shift indicators provide urgency states and configurable thresholds, supporting both aggressive and conservative driving styles.

Demo/Test Mode

Built-in scenarios let you preview behavior, validate widget logic, and showcase the app without requiring a live adapter session.

Transparent Synthetic Sources

When a value is calculated or inferred, GaugeKing surfaces source details so users understand what is measured versus estimated.
